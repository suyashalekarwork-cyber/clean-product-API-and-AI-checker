You are re-extracting structured fields from a Fareharbor tour/activity's raw description text.

Field definitions:
- redo_desc_about: The general narrative that describes WHAT THE EXPERIENCE IS — the overview or "about the experience" text. Typically flowing prose introducing the activity. If the raw text has a "description:" label or an ##About header, its content goes here.
- redo_desc_highlights: Specific, concrete selling points that would make a customer choose THIS experience over others — unique features, standout moments, named attractions, distinctive elements, or award-winning aspects. If the raw text has a "highlights:" label or ##Highlights header, its content goes here. The label is the tiebreaker: labeled content ALWAYS goes to highlights even if it reads like narrative.
- redo_desc_what_included: Items, services, or amenities provided at no extra cost, even if it says "Journey Inclusions" or "Package Inclusions" or "Tour Includes".
- redo_desc_what_excluded: Items, services, or costs explicitly NOT included in the base price. Look for phrases like "at your own expense", "not included", "additional cost", "extra charge", "optional add-on at extra cost". An "Optional Add-Ons" section listing upgrades available at extra cost = what_excluded.
- redo_desc_itinerary: The ordered route or schedule of the experience, for display as a list of short rows. Each row is one stop or one event.
  QUALIFIES ONLY IF it is already a list of at least 2 entries, in order, AND at least one of these is true:
    (a) at least 2 entries LEAD WITH a clock time -- "9am", "11:30", "2.00 PM", "16h00", "14:30", "10.15am". A short label before the time is fine ("Boarding: 11:30 am"). 24-hour and non-English formats count.
    (b) at least 2 entries LEAD WITH a step marker -- "1.", "2.", "Day 2", "Night 1", "Stop 3".
    (c) there are 3 or more entries and EVERY row is a short stop name or a short action -- "Circular Quay", "Safety briefing", "Depart for the fishing grounds". If any row is a full sentence or a paragraph, (c) does not apply.
  An entry is one line, or one sentence within a line. Marked entries must be at least half of all entries.
  THE MARKER MUST BEGIN THE ENTRY. A clock time appearing inside a sentence does not qualify the field -- "you will receive notification by 6pm the evening before" is not a schedule row.
  NEVER QUALIFIES: flowing prose or paragraphs; ordering words alone ("then", "next", "first", "finally"); places named inside a sentence ("Join our guides through the trails to 2 lookouts, the Maori carvings and Omanawa falls" names four stops in order and is still description); selling points and adjectives ("Relaxed, supportive vibe", "Massive fun", "Professional and well-organised", "Small groups").
  CONTENT TEST: the steps must describe THE EXPERIENCE ITSELF. Parking, key drop-off, equipment collection, contacting the operator and other booking admin are NOT an itinerary even when numbered. Those go to redo_desc_about.
  BEFORE APPLYING THIS TEST, move opening-hours and departure-day lines -- "Available 11:30 am - 2:45 pm Monday - Saturday", "Departures run Thursday to Sunday at 5:30pm" -- to redo_desc_about. They say WHEN the experience runs, not what happens during it.
  IF IT DOES NOT QUALIFY, leave this field EMPTY and keep the text in redo_desc_about. Never delete it.
  A list of product OPTIONS or choices (e.g. Option A / Option B / Option C) is NOT an itinerary -- put that in redo_desc_what_included or redo_desc_other.
- redo_desc_what_to_bring: A list of things the customer must bring or wear, even if it says "Please Bring" or appears in a "More Info" section.
- redo_desc_duration_text: How long the experience lasts, as stated in the text.
- redo_desc_requirements: Age limits, height/weight minimums, health warnings, fitness level requirements, even if buried inside a "Tour Requirements" or "Please Note" section. When content is ambiguous: if it restricts WHO can participate (age, height, weight, health, fitness) or what they MUST do or have before participating — it is requirements. If it simply describes the experience or recommends something, it belongs in redo_desc_about.
- redo_desc_cancellation: Refund rules, cancellation windows, rebooking fees, weather-cancellation policy, no-show policy.
- redo_desc_check_in: Instructions about arrival or check-in stated in the description text.
- redo_min_age: The minimum age requirement, sourced specifically from an embedded "min_age:" label when present. If no such label exists, leave "".
- redo_max_age: The maximum age requirement, sourced specifically from an embedded "max_age:" label when present. If no such label exists, leave "".
- redo_group_size: The group size limit, sourced specifically from an embedded "group_size:" label when present. If no such label exists, leave "".
- redo_meeting_point: The meeting point / starting location, sourced specifically from an embedded "meeting_point:" label when present. If no such label exists, leave "".
- redo_desc_other: This field is NARROW. It accepts ONLY these three kinds of content:
   1. Pricing, rates, rate cards, and price lists
   2. Booking, payment, and reservation instructions (how to book, payment methods, deposit terms, availability enquiries)
   3. Promotional cross-sell to the operator's OTHER products or tours (e.g. "see you next time on our Hunter Valley tour")
  Nothing else belongs here. Do NOT put general narrative, descriptive prose, supplier background, testimonials, or unclassifiable text in redo_desc_other.
