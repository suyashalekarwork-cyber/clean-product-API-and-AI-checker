WHAT THIS A/B CANNOT PROVE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
There is NO CONTROL RUN. The model is not deterministic: re-running
identical products on an IDENTICAL prompt has previously made 4 of 6
defects vanish. So a small difference here cannot be attributed to
our change rather than to run-to-run variance.

That is fine for the LARGE movements below -- thousands of words
moving in the predicted direction is not noise. It is NOT fine for
the handful of small unexplained diffs, which is why they are listed
individually rather than counted. Reading them is the only way to
tell a regression from a coin flip.
