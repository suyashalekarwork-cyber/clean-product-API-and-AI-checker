                the checker compared URLs exactly and a long URL differed
                in its tail. Fixed, and the fix is why this reads 5 not 6.

  NO HEADING    ~2 of 6 sampled survived. EXPECT ROUGHLY A THIRD TO BE
                REAL. This check asks 'did a heading license this fill?'
                and answers using our stem list -- which cannot name every
                supplier wording. Confirmed false positives: 'Numbers on
                the Day' (licenses group_size), 'Session Length'
                (duration), 'Gift eCards Available' (extras), and a block
                of FAQ questions used as headings. All four ARE headings;
                our stems simply do not list them. CLAUDE.md already
                records that this mapper cannot be completed -- adding
                patterns for every topic wording is classification by
                meaning, the thing heading-gating replaced.
                Genuine examples that DID survive: PQC41U (content under
                'Meeting Time' went to check_in, not meeting_point) and
                P4RRV9 (highlights filled with no highlights heading).

  Two detector bugs were found and fixed by this verification, BEFORE
  publishing: the licensing check used the router (first stem wins), so a
  compound heading like 'Important Health and Safety Information' licensed
  important_info and left health_safety reading as unlicensed; and the URL
  check compared exactly rather than by prefix.

What each type means
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  CONTENT LOSS   raw text that reached no field at all. Shown with the
                 heading it sat under, so it can be put back rather than
                 merely counted.
  REWORDED       present, but not word-for-word. The model paraphrased.
  URL LOST       a link in the raw absent from the output.
  INVENTED       4+ consecutive output words that appear nowhere in raw.
  MID-SENTENCE   a field value starting part-way through a sentence.
  NO HEADING     a field filled when no heading or label names it. This
                 is the failure heading-gating exists to prevent.
  DUPLICATED     the same sentence in 2+ fields. Reported, never removed.
  CONTAMINATION  our own prompt example text appearing in real output.
