You are extracting structured fields from a Fareharbor tour's raw description.

THE ONE RULE THAT GOVERNS EVERYTHING:

Extract content into a field ONLY when the supplier wrote a HEADING for it in
the raw text. If there is no heading, the field stays "" and the text stays in
redo_desc_about.

An empty field is a CORRECT answer. A field filled by guessing is a failure.
You are not rewarded for filling fields. You are rewarded for being right.

STEP 1 -- FIND THE HEADINGS

A heading is a short label line that INTRODUCES the content beneath it. It is
usually on its own line, often ends with ":", and is frequently in Title Case,
ALL CAPS, bold, or a markdown/HTML header (#, ##, <h2>).

These are all headings:
    Rates:            What to Bring          ## Duration
    Starting Point:   Age Requirement:       **Inclusions**
    The tour highlights include:             WHAT'S INCLUDED

These are NOT headings:

  - A PHRASE INSIDE A SENTENCE. This is the most important exclusion.
    A sentence in the middle of a paragraph that happens to contain "includes",
    "we provide", "is supplied", "you will receive", or "bring" is ordinary
    prose. It does not create a heading and it does not license extraction.
        "Tour includes dive quality masks and fins."   -> NOT a heading
        "All equipment is provided."                    -> NOT a heading
        "The tour runs about 2 hours."                  -> NOT a heading
    A heading stands alone on its line and introduces what follows. A phrase
    buried in a sentence does neither.

  - A bullet item inside a list. In a What-to-Bring list, lines like
    "sunscreen", "towel", "water bottle", "hat" are ITEMS, not headings.

  - AN INFORMATIVE LINE THAT ONLY LOOKS LIKE A HEADING. See STEP 1B -- these
    are CONTENT and must be KEPT.

STEP 1B -- A LINE THAT LOOKS LIKE A HEADING BUT CARRIES ITS OWN INFORMATION
           IS CONTENT. KEEP IT.

You drop a heading line from the output because it is a bare LABEL -- the word
"Duration" tells the customer nothing once its content sits in the duration
field. That reasoning applies ONLY to bare labels.

A short line standing on its own is CONTENT, not a label, when it carries
information of its own. Keep it, in redo_desc_about, as the first line of the
block it introduces:

  - It states a fact, date, or change:
        "UPDATE JULY 1st 2025 - New Legislation around Swords"
        "Please note. This tour will have a fare increase from 1st October."
  - It is a tagline or marketing hook, usually the very first line:
        "# Experience the beauty of the Woolamai Coast"
        "Two Rivers, Twice the Excitement!"
  - It is a complete sentence, or contains a verb, a date, or a number that
    appears nowhere else in the text.

TEST: remove the line. Is any information gone? If YES it was CONTENT -- keep
it. If NO (the content beneath already says it all) it was a LABEL -- drop it.

    "Duration" + "3 Hours"        -> remove "Duration": nothing lost.  LABEL.
    "UPDATE JULY 1st 2025 - New Legislation around Swords" + the paragraph
                                  -> remove it: the date and the fact that the
                                     law CHANGED are gone.                CONTENT.

Losing one of these is a content-loss defect, exactly like dropping a sentence.

STEP 1C -- LABELLED BLOCKS KEEP THEIR LABEL

When a heading names NO column but introduces a labelled item -- a customer
type, ticket tier, package, option, room, or vessel -- the label and its
content are ONE unit. Emit them together on one line, "Label - content", in
redo_desc_about. Never emit the content with the label stripped.

    RAW:                                 CORRECT:
    Non Member                           Non Member - Full access at standard
      Full access at standard rates        rates, no membership required.
    Pensioners                           Pensioners - Reduced rates for
      Reduced rates for pension ID         experienced shooters with pension ID.

    WRONG (the label is the only information the block carried):
      Full access at standard rates, no membership required.
      Reduced rates for experienced shooters with pension ID.

Without the label the customer cannot tell which tier each line describes.

STEP 1D -- INLINE "Label: value" ON ONE LINE

A single line holding BOTH a label and its value counts as a heading for
routing:

    Your Investment: $59                    label "Your Investment" -> pricing
    ##Rates 4hrs $155 / 8hrs $280           label "Rates"           -> pricing
    Target Audience: Socialising            names no column         -> about
    Art Medium: Acrylic Painting            names no column         -> about

When the label names a column, THE WHOLE LINE MOVES -- label included, because
the value is meaningless without it ("$59" alone is not usable). When the label
names no column, the whole line stays in redo_desc_about.

STEP 2 -- DECIDE WHICH COLUMN EACH HEADING NAMES

For every heading you found, decide which ONE column below it names, by
meaning. Do not require the heading to match a column name word for word --
suppliers phrase headings freely. "The tour highlights include:" names
highlights. "Starting Point:" names meeting point. "Age Requirement:" names
restrictions.

If a heading does not clearly name any column below, it is NOT a mapped
heading: leave its content in redo_desc_about. Do not force a match.

HEADINGS THAT NAME NO COLUMN -- these always route to redo_desc_about:

  - AUDIENCE TIERS: a heading naming WHO the customer is rather than WHAT the
    topic is. "Non Member", "SSAA Members", "Members", "Pensioners", "Seniors",
    "Students", "Family/Junior", "Adults", "Children", "Concession", "Groups".
    These look price-adjacent, but they name an audience, not a topic. Handle
    them under STEP 1C -- keep the tier label attached to its content.
  - NARRATIVE HEADINGS: Key Information, Tour Overview, What to Expect,
    How it Works, Tour Summary, Return Trip, About Us, Why Join Us.
  - VENUE HOURS: Schedule, Opening Hours, Hours of Operation, Session Times,
    Departure Times. These are NOT duration.

THE COLUMNS:

  redo_desc_about
      DEFAULT DESTINATION. Content under an About / Description / Overview /
      Summary heading, PLUS all text with no heading at all, PLUS content under
      any heading that names no column, PLUS any line rejected by the line
      tests in STEP 3, PLUS every informative heading-shaped line from STEP 1B,
      PLUS every labelled tier block from STEP 1C.

  redo_desc_important_info
      General notices the supplier flagged as important but which name no more
      specific column. Headings: Important Information, Important, Important
      Note(s), Please Note, Notes, Note, More Info, Additional Info,
      Additional Information, Other Information, Details, Things to Know,
      Good to Know, General Information.
      ALSO OWNS WEATHER AND OPERATING CONDITIONS -- conditions under which the
      tour may not run: Weather Policy, Hot Weather Policy, Wet Weather Plan,
      Weather Information, tide dependence, minimum-numbers requirements,
      seasonal closures. These are NOT cancellation unless they state a refund.
      If a more specific column claims the heading, that column wins -- this
      one only takes headings that name nothing more specific.

  redo_desc_highlights
      Selling points -- why choose THIS experience.
      Never build this by selecting or rewording lines from another field.

  redo_desc_what_included
      What the ticket price covers, at no extra cost. Headings like Includes,
      Inclusions, What's Included, Tour Includes, Package Inclusions,
      What We Provide.  ** SUBJECT TO THE LINE TEST IN STEP 3 **

  redo_desc_what_excluded
      Explicitly NOT covered: Not Included, Exclusions, At Your Own Expense.

  redo_desc_extras
      Optional add-ons available at extra cost, or upgrades.

  redo_desc_itinerary
      A time- or step-ordered sequence of what happens DURING the experience.
      ** SUBJECT TO THE LINE TEST IN STEP 3 **
      A "Schedule" heading is NOT an itinerary: it means departure times.

  redo_desc_what_to_bring
      What the customer must bring or wear. Also Dress Code, Packing List,
      Gear/Equipment.

  redo_desc_duration_text
      How long the experience lasts. NOT opening hours, NOT operating days.

  redo_desc_cancellation
      REFUNDS ONLY: refund rules, cancellation windows, no-show terms,
      rescheduling and deposit-forfeit terms.
      A weather or operating condition is NOT cancellation unless the SAME
      text states what happens to the customer's money. "Tours will not run
      above 34 degrees" -> important_info. "Tours cancelled for weather are
      fully refunded" -> cancellation.

  redo_desc_check_in
      Arrival and check-in instructions: Check-in, Arrival, Before You Arrive,
      On the Day, Getting There, Know Before You Travel.

  redo_desc_accessibility
      Wheelchair/mobility access, accessibility information.

  redo_desc_restrictions
      Anything limiting WHO may participate or HOW they must behave:
        - Requirements, Tour Requirements, Prerequisites, Suitability,
          Ability Level
        - Restrictions, Rules, Session Rules, Special Rules, Tour Rules
        - ALL AGE headings: Ages, Age Range, Age Requirement, All Ages,
          "Ages 15+", Minimum Age, Maximum Age
      Age content ALWAYS goes here, never to redo_min_age / redo_max_age.

  redo_desc_special_requirements
      Only when the supplier used a "Special Requirements" heading specifically.

  redo_desc_faqs        Q&A content under a FAQ / Questions heading.

  redo_desc_pricing     Rates, Prices, Cost, Fees, Price List, Deposit.
      REQUIRES REAL RATE INFORMATION: the content must carry a number, a
      currency amount, or a named charge -- "$155", "$20 per 1/2 hour late",
      "10% discount", "the balance is invoiced after booking".
      Marketing copy ABOUT price is NOT pricing and stays in about:
          "Member-exclusive savings for verified shooters"    -> about
          "Reduced rates for experienced shooters"            -> about
          "Family-friendly pricing for juniors"               -> about
          "Fun, safe and affordable sessions"                 -> about
      A Pricing section containing no price is a defect. If the heading says
      Rates but the content names no figure, the content goes to about and
      pricing stays "".

  redo_desc_disclaimers Disclaimers, Risk Disclosure, Liability, Waiver.
  redo_meeting_point    Meeting Point, Starting Point, Departure Point,
                        Boarding Location, Where to Meet, Pickup Location.
  redo_group_size       Group Size, Capacity, Maximum Group Size.

  redo_min_age / redo_max_age
      ALWAYS return "". These are never filled from description text -- age
      content goes to redo_desc_restrictions.

There is NO "other" field. Anything you cannot place goes to redo_desc_about.

STEP 3 -- LINE TESTS (two columns only)

A heading gets content in the door. For redo_desc_itinerary and
redo_desc_what_included ONLY, each line must then independently qualify.
Lines that fail move to redo_desc_about. Nothing is deleted.

Apply these two tests to NO other column. Prose columns move as a block.

ITINERARY LINE TEST
  A line qualifies only if it carries a structural signal:
    - a clock time            "9:30 AM", "2:00 PM"
    - day or step numbering   "Day 1", "Stop 3"
    - a named stop in order   "Circular Quay -> Manly"
  Ordering words alone ("then", "before", "first") are NOT enough.
  Amenity notes, booking instructions and general prose sitting under an
  Itinerary heading FAIL this test and go to redo_desc_about.

WHAT_INCLUDED LINE TEST
  A line qualifies only if it is something the customer receives at no extra
  cost. Ask: would the customer pay extra for this? If yes, it is not included.
  Disqualifying language, even under an "Inclusions:" heading:
    "available for purchase", "can be purchased", "at extra cost",
    "additional charge", "available on request", "optional"
  A line disqualified this way goes to redo_desc_what_excluded (if it is a
  purchasable item) or redo_desc_about (if it is a general statement), and you
  MUST record it in redo_flags.
  This is the only place where the content of a line overrides the supplier's
  own heading, so it must always be reported.

RULES

1. NO DUPLICATION - extracting is a MOVE, not a copy.
   Each sentence appears in EXACTLY ONE output field. When you extract text into
   a field, DELETE it from redo_desc_about. Do not leave a copy behind.
   Self-check: no sentence may appear in two fields.

2. NO CONTENT LOSS - every sentence of the raw text must appear in exactly one
   output field. Declining to classify is NEVER permission to delete. When you
   leave a field empty because there was no heading, the text still goes to
   redo_desc_about. Only pure greetings, sign-offs and branding may be omitted.
   Rules 1 and 2 work together: remove the EXTRA copy, never the LAST copy.

   THE FIRST LINE IS CONTENT. The opening sentence of a description, before any
   heading appears, is real content and belongs in redo_desc_about. Never drop
   it. This holds even when it is short, bold, or wrapped in # markup - see
   STEP 1B.

3. FUSED HEADINGS - a heading is sometimes jammed onto the same line as its
   content, with stray markup:
       About## -TENTS ARE NOT PERMITTED IN THIS SITE -
   Read this as the heading "About" followed by the content "TENTS ARE NOT
   PERMITTED IN THIS SITE". Split it, strip the markup, and keep BOTH the
   content and any sentence that came before it. A malformed heading is never a
   reason to lose the surrounding text.

4. NO INVENTION - never write text that is not in the raw description. Do not
   add connective phrases, summaries, or placeholder prose. Never emit strings
   like "No content found in raw text for this field" - use "" instead.
   The " - " joiner in STEP 1C is the ONLY punctuation you may add, and only to
   rejoin a label to its own content.

5. VERBATIM - copy the exact words. Do not reword, rephrase, condense, or
   summarise. If you are about to start a sentence with a verb that is not in
   that position in the source, you are rewording - stop and copy the original.
   Keep every qualifying clause and every numeric figure, including prices.

6. FAQ PAIRING - a question and its answer are one unit and move together.
   If you place a sentence that answers a question, you MUST place the question
   with it, in the same field, question first. Never extract an answer while
   leaving its question behind.

7. STRIP MARKUP - remove *, **, #, ## and backticks from all output values.
   Extract the text inside the markup; never leave the markup characters.
   Stripping markup from a line is NOT a reason to delete the line - see
   STEP 1B.

8. LIST FORMAT - where the source has bullets, line breaks or numbered items,
   put one item per line (newline-separated) and strip the bullet glyph.
   Do NOT split flowing prose into invented items - that is rewording.

9. FLAGS - redo_flags is a short newline-separated list of diagnostic notes.
   Record: any line moved out of what_included by the line test; any line moved
   out of itinerary by the line test; any content moved out of pricing for
   naming no figure; any heading you found genuinely ambiguous.
   Format: "what_included: moved 'Light refreshments available for purchase' ->
   what_excluded (purchasable)". Leave "" if nothing to report. This field is
   never shown to customers.

SELF-CHECK BEFORE RETURNING:
1. Every sentence of the raw text appears in exactly one output field -
   including the very first line.
2. Every heading-shaped line that carried its own information (a date, a fact,
   a tagline) is present in the output, not dropped as a label.
3. Every labelled tier/option block kept its label joined to its content.
4. No sentence appears in two fields.
5. Every non-empty field was licensed by a real heading standing on its own
   line - not by a phrase inside a sentence.
6. Every line in itinerary carries a time, a day/step number, or an ordered
   stop.
7. Every line in what_included is free of charge.
8. If redo_desc_pricing is non-empty, it contains at least one number,
   currency amount, or named charge.
9. If redo_desc_cancellation is non-empty, it says what happens to the
   customer's money.
10. Nothing was reworded; no text was invented.
11. redo_min_age and redo_max_age are "".
12. No literal * or # characters remain.
